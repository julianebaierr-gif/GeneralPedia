Rogue Credit Union: Rules, Limits, Tax Rates & Strategy

Explore Rogue Credit Union membership rules, personal loan limits, savings dividend rates, and digital banking tools for account holders.

Sarah Mitchell
September 17, 2026
6 min read

What Is Rogue Credit Union?

Rogue Credit Union is a member-owned financial cooperative headquartered in Medford, Oregon, serving southern and western Oregon communities. Founded in 1956 by a small group of local teachers, it now holds over $3 billion in assets and serves more than 200,000 member-owners.

Unlike commercial banks that answer to outside shareholders, Rogue operates as a non-profit entity. Surplus earnings return directly to members through higher savings yields, lower loan rates, and reduced account fees.

Understanding how Rogue operates helps Oregon residents choose the right local financial institution. The credit union offers traditional deposit accounts alongside auto loans, mortgages, and business banking services.

Rogue Credit Union Routing Number and Core Account Details

Managing direct deposits, wire transfers, and automated bill payments requires accurate routing details. Using the correct wire transfer code prevents transaction delays and rejected transfers.

The official Rogue Credit Union routing number for all electronic transactions is 321179477. Members must enter this nine-digit ABA number alongside their individual account numbers when setting up direct deposits.

For external transfers, using this number ensures funds clear within standard ACH processing windows. Double-checking account numbers alongside the routing code protects your money from processing errors.

Deposit Safety: Is Rogue Credit Union FDIC Insured?

Consumers often ask if their deposits carry federal backing outside the traditional commercial banking network. The short answer is that Rogue carries equivalent federal protection through a dedicated credit union regulator.

Rogue Credit Union is not FDIC insured because the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation only covers commercial banks. Instead, member deposits receive insurance through the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA), backed by the full faith and credit of the United States government.

The National Credit Union Share Insurance Fund (NCUSIF) protects individual member accounts up to $250,000. Joint accounts, retirement funds, and trust deposits carry separate coverage limits based on ownership structures.

Rogue Credit Union Auto Loan Rates and Financing Terms

Vehicle financing remains one of Rogue's most competitive product lines for local buyers. The credit union funds new auto purchases, used car transactions, and high-rate auto loan refinances.

Rogue Credit Union auto loan rates feature competitive fixed annual percentage rates (APRs) tied to credit profiles, loan terms, and vehicle age. Members often receive a 0.25% interest rate discount by setting up automatic loan payments from an active Rogue checking account.

Borrowers can secure pre-approval online or inside local branches prior to visiting car dealerships. Pre-approval gives buyers clear budget limits and bargaining leverage when negotiating vehicle purchase prices.

Rogue Credit Union Locations in Oregon

Physical branch access remains essential for cash deposits, notary services, and face-to-face loan consultations. Rogue maintains a robust footprint across Pacific Northwest communities.

Rogue Credit Union locations in Oregon span Jackson, Josephine, Klamath, Curry, Coos, and Douglas counties. Main branch clusters sit in Medford, Ashland, Grants Pass, Roseburg, Klamath Falls, and Coos Bay.

Members who travel out of state can access thousands of surcharge-free ATMs nationwide through the CO-OP ATM Network. Shared branching partnerships allow Rogue members to conduct basic teller transactions at participating partner credit unions across the country.

Rogue Credit Union vs Oregon State Credit Union

Choosing between regional financial cooperatives comes down to geographic footprint, member rewards, and loan options. Both institutions serve Oregon residents with distinct regional focuses.

Nationwide peers like Eastman Credit Union in Tennessee or Summit Federal Credit Union in New York operate on similar regional cooperative models. Comparing regional credit unions shows that localized focus delivers better customer care than large national banks.

How to Join Rogue Credit Union

Joining a credit union requires meeting field-of-membership qualifications defined by geographical boundaries or organizational affiliations. Rogue keeps its membership path accessible to most regional residents.

Learning how to join Rogue Credit Union involves confirming live, work, worship, or school ties within its Oregon service territory. Immediate family members of current Rogue cardholders automatically qualify for membership regardless of where they reside.

Prospective members can apply online in under ten minutes or visit any physical branch location. Opening a membership requires a valid state ID, Social Security number, and a $5 initial deposit into a basic membership share savings account.

Rogue Credit Union Customer Service Phone Number and Support Channels

Direct access to member services helps resolve account inquiries, card blocks, and wire issues quickly. Rogue provides multiple support channels across digital and voice platforms.

The official Rogue Credit Union customer service phone number is 1-800-856-7328. Representatives handle account inquiries, loan application updates, and card services Monday through Saturday during regular business hours.

Members can also access automated account balances using phone banking or send secure messages through online banking dashboards. Mobile app tools allow immediate debit card locking if a card is misplaced or stolen.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Rogue Credit Union routing number?

The routing number for Rogue Credit Union is 321179477. This nine-digit number processes all ACH transfers, direct deposits, and incoming wire transactions.

Is Rogue Credit Union FDIC insured?

No, Rogue Credit Union is federally insured by the NCUA rather than the FDIC. Member deposits carry full federal protection up to $250,000 per individual account holder.

Who is eligible to join Rogue Credit Union?

Anyone living, working, worshipping, or attending school in Oregon's select southern and coastal counties qualifies for membership alongside immediate family of current members.

How do I reach Rogue Credit Union customer service?

Call 1-800-856-7328 to speak with member services representatives. You can also use secure live chat features inside the mobile app and online banking portal.

Does Rogue Credit Union offer shared branching?

Yes, Rogue belongs to the CO-OP Shared Branch Network. Members can complete teller transactions at thousands of participating credit union locations nationwide.

Where is Rogue Credit Union headquartered?

Rogue Credit Union is headquartered in Medford, Oregon, operating numerous branches across Jackson, Josephine, Klamath, Douglas, Curry, and Coos counties.
